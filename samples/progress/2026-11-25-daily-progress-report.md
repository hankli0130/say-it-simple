## Review

如果你是要**整理資料給客戶**，最後一句可以換成 `My next step is to put together our questions and gather the data our client needs for M4.`

原文有兩個用法值得記：

- `for Brushmo`：Brushmo 是專案名稱，直接用名字即可。`the M3 deployment for Brushmo` 清楚交代這是哪個專案的部署。
- `I’m working on the M4 deployment.`：表達「正在做某項工作」時，用 **work on + 任務**。加上 `now`，就能自然接續上一句的 `yesterday`。

**口說練習**｜只看 `M3 done → M4 → client questions → data`，試著說：

> Yesterday, I finished the M3 deployment for Brushmo. I’m working on M4 now. Next, I’ll list the questions we need to ask our client and figure out what data we need from them.

**會議追問**  
*What do you need from the client for M4?*  
*I’m still putting that list together. It’ll cover the questions we need them to answer and the data we need them to share.*
